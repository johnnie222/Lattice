# Lattice product brief

## Positioning

**See the market. See your portfolio. Understand what changed.**

Lattice is not intended to be another generic investing toolbox. Its advantage is a single visual language that moves naturally from:

**market -> sector/theme -> stock -> personal portfolio**

The product wins when a user can understand the important state faster than with lists, tables and charts.

## Target user

Self-directed investors and active market followers who:
- look at US markets frequently
- want a fast visual overview
- care about sectors/themes and market breadth
- want to understand their own portfolio in market context
- do not need a full professional terminal every time they check the market

## Core jobs to be done

1. “Show me what is moving.”
2. “Show me where strength and weakness are.”
3. “Show me what happened to my portfolio.”
4. “Show me what actually drove my day.”
5. “Tell me what changed since I last looked.”
6. “Tell me what matters before the next session.”

## Differentiation

Heatmaps already exist. Lattice should not claim that heatmaps themselves are unique.

The differentiation is the combination of:
- premium mobile-first visual execution
- market/sector/theme drill-down in one map language
- personal portfolio heatmap
- portfolio contribution and benchmark context
- state-change awareness
- concise personalized daily briefing

A competitor can add a heatmap without reproducing the whole product model.

## Competitive learning

The category has proven consumer demand. Existing heatmap apps can reach meaningful download volume even with dated visual design.

Useful observed customer complaints/requests in the category:
- intrusive ads
- inability to view different periods
- missing dollar + percent context
- requests for custom portfolio heatmaps
- requests for widgets
- requests for more markets

Product response:
- no ads
- historical periods are launch-priority
- portfolio heatmap/personalization stays central
- widget is later
- more markets are demand-led, not launch scope

## Monetization hypothesis

Free install with a useful core experience.

**Pro hypothesis**
- about $1.99/month
- about $14.99/year
- annual highlighted
- community/launch offer around $9.99 first year

Likely Pro value:
- portfolio intelligence
- history
- personalized close/briefing
- advanced retention features
- later AI/news intelligence

Do not build a paywall that makes the free app useless.

## Success metrics

The important early metrics are not raw downloads alone.

Track:
- install -> first useful map interaction
- portfolio creation rate
- D1 / D7 / D30 retention
- share of users who return around open/close
- free -> trial/paywall -> paid conversion
- annual vs monthly mix
- cancellation/renewal
- usage of My Portfolio Today and Lattice Close
- share-card creation rate
- notification open rate once alerts exist

## Launch philosophy

Launch before the full vision is complete.

A strong V1 should prove:
- people want the visual product
- people create portfolios
- personalized portfolio intelligence improves retention
- some users will pay annually

Then let usage data determine whether the next dollar goes to alerts, AI/news, widgets, additional markets or something else.
